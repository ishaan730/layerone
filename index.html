Services in detail

How an engagement runs

Every engagement starts with an audit, because the right answer depends on where your system is now. After that we build with your team, not for it.

  1. Audit

    Typically 2–3 weeks

    We look at your files, your code and how work actually moves between teams. You get a written report and a ranked list of what to fix first.

  2. Decide

    Typically 1–2 weeks

    We agree the structure with your design and engineering leads: token layers, naming, what belongs in the core and who owns what.

  3. Build

    Ongoing, in releases

    We build alongside your team, in your Figma files and your repository, and ship in releases your teams can plan around.

  4. Hand over

    Last month of the engagement

    Documentation, training and a governance model your team can run without us. Success is how little you need us at the end.

What we believe about systems

Learned the slow way, at companies with dozens of designers, hundreds of engineers and more than one country to keep consistent.

  • A system is an agreement, not a library.

    Components are the easy part. Ownership, contribution and release cadence are what keep a system alive once it's out in the world.

  • Tie it to what the business is trying to do.

    Systems keep their funding when they show time saved and cost avoided. Usage counts alone don't make that case.

  • Build where the system will live.

    We work in your files and your codebase, next to your designers and engineers. Nothing is designed in isolation and thrown over a wall.

  • Every decision comes with a reason.

    Scored, written down and visible to everyone, so a no is understood and a yes can be planned around.

  • Accessible by default.

    Contrast and responsive behaviour are built into the tokens from day one, not patched in after an audit.

  • Leave the team able to run it.

    Documentation, training and governance are part of the work. We measure ourselves by how little you need us at the end.

Questions

If yours isn't here, ask it on a call or by email.

Do you design products or marketing websites?

No. We only work on design systems and the shared UI infrastructure around them: tokens, components, patterns, documentation and governance.

Can you work with the system we already have?

Yes, and most of our work is exactly that. We start with an audit of what exists and keep everything that's working.

Do you work with engineers directly?

Always. A design system that only lives in Figma isn't a system. We pair with your engineers on token pipelines and component APIs, and we can build components in React, Vue or web components when you need extra hands.

Do you support multiple brands, products or countries?

Yes. Multi-brand token architecture is one of our specialities. At CARS24 the same token system runs 10+ product lines across India, the UAE and Australia, with each theme overriding only what it needs.

How long does it take?

An audit typically takes two to three weeks. Building or restructuring a system usually runs for three to six months, depending on scope and how many teams are involved. We agree the plan before we start.

Which tools do you work in?

Figma, Storybook, Tokens Studio and Style Dictionary are common, but the structure we design isn't tied to a tool. We work in whatever your teams already use.

Why monthly instead of a fixed project fee?

Design systems are products, not projects. They keep changing as your company does. A monthly engagement lets the scope follow what you actually need, and you can pause or stop at any time.

Tell us where your system is today.

Thirty minutes is enough to tell whether we're the right fit. If we're not, we'll say so and point you somewhere better.
