CARS24 · 2025 · Patterns, CMS, Adoption

Pages built from tested blocks, not from scratch

CARS24 was turning into a super app and launching new services almost every month. Every homepage and landing page was designed and built from zero. We built a pattern library that lets teams assemble pages from tested blocks, so they ship faster and every page feels like one product.

The 30-second version

The problem

Every new page started from a blank canvas. The same cards and carousels were built again and again, and no two services felt alike.

What we did

Six families of page blocks with grid, text, image and behaviour rules, wired to the CMS and the tokens, with one name from Figma to code.

What changed

Pages took about 43% less time. Within three months every major redesign in three countries was built on the library.

Every page started from a blank canvas

Nothing was shared, so the same work was repeated, it cost more, took longer, and moving between services felt like changing apps.

03

Borrowing what people already know

Our users also lived in Swiggy, Zepto and Blinkit. Service grids became quick-action rows, offer banners became the banner family, scrolling rails became sliders.

Read the detail

We audited homepages, landing pages and exit flows in every market and sat with product managers, engineers and visual designers. Layouts were all over the place, one component existed in many builds, there were no rules for content, and nobody knew how a section should adapt across screens.

05

Six families of page blocks

Built on the base design system, with layout templates, ready-made combinations, tokens, specs and usage guidance.

Banners

One template, three types: full bleed, padded and carousel, in fixed sizes. Used for campaigns, offers and the top of every homepage.

Grids

Two, three and four columns in landscape, square or portrait tiles, with one header and one button. Cars by body type, explore by brand.

Sliders

Rails that scroll sideways with one to four cards in view, always with the next card peeking in. Hot deals, trending new cars.

Lists

For content that needs more words than a tile: thumbnail, title, a line of text, a chevron. Showrooms, car checks.

Stories and video

Story bubbles and video cards with autoplay set in the CMS. Customer stories, expert videos.

Snackbars and drop-off cards

Small nudges that bring people back to what they left: a car they viewed, a loan application they didn't finish.

06

One name from Figma to code to CMS

The library only works if four groups can use it without waiting on each other. Every pattern has one name everywhere.
