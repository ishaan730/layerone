Work

Three case studies from one company, CARS24, that follow a design system from its foundations to how it's run to how teams adopt it. Each one starts with the problem and ends with what changed, in numbers.

  • Foundations at CARS24

    One colour system for 10+ products in three countries

    CARS24 grew from one product to more than six across India, the UAE and Australia, and every team had built its own colours. We rebuilt the palette, designed a four-layer token architecture and gave every product one source of truth. The palette became the colour of the CARS24 rebrand.

    65%faster design-to-development handoff

  • Operations at CARS24

    How the design system team stopped being the bottleneck

    Every team needed something from the design system, and one small team couldn't keep up. Requests arrived by DM, decisions felt random and teams built around the system. We designed one front door, a 70-point scorecard anyone can apply, three ownership tiers and a contribution playbook.

    70 ptsscorecard that replaced the loudest-voice backlog

  • Adoption at CARS24

    Pages built from tested blocks, not from scratch

    CARS24 was becoming a super app and launching new services almost every month, and every homepage and landing page started from a blank canvas. We built a pattern library of page blocks with content rules, wired to the CMS and the design tokens, so teams assemble pages instead of designing them.

    ~43%less time to design and build a new page

These projects were led by Layer One's founder as Design Systems Lead at CARS24, working with CARS24's design, engineering, brand and content teams. Images belong to CARS24.
