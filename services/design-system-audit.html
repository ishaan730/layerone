questions:

What does a design system audit include?

An inventory of every style, token and component in Figma and code, a scan for hard-coded values and duplicated components, a WCAG contrast check of key flows, a maturity score and a ranked roadmap, presented in a 60-minute readout.

How long does a design system audit take?

Two weeks, at a fixed price of $1,500. We need access to your Figma files and repositories and about three hours of your leads' time.

Do we need a design system already?

No. If you don't have one, the audit inventories what your products use today, which is the starting point for building one.

What happens after the audit?

You can act on the roadmap yourselves, or continue with a Foundations build. The audit fee is credited in full if you start Foundations within 60 days.
