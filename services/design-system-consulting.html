questions:

What does a design system consultant do?

Helps a team build, run and scale its design system: setting up governance and contribution, building components and patterns, running releases, and measuring adoption, with or without an in-house system team.

How is Partner different from hiring?

You get senior design system experience about two days a week from day one, without a hiring process, and can pause or stop with notice before the next billing date.

Can you work with our existing system team?

Yes. Most Partner engagements support an existing team: we take on governance, tooling or specific components while your team keeps ownership.

How much does design system consulting cost?

$2,900 a month, billed in advance, with no minimum beyond the first month. Founding partners in 2027 pay $2,320.
