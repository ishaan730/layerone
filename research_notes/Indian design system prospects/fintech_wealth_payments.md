## Which companies (2015-2026) have significant funding/revenue/headcount, non-household, with accessible product?

### Takeaway
Strongest verified PRIMARY candidates so far: Cashfree Payments (free sandbox dashboard), Easebuzz (payments, profitable FY25), INDmoney, Smallcase, Stable Money, Wint Wealth, Vyapar. Fyle status is unclear (possible acquisition). Remaining named candidates are unverified leads.
