# Indian consumer technology prospects for Layer One (design-systems consulting)

Method note: ~13 searches only (budget cap). Financials below come from search-result summaries of the cited articles; I did not open every article. Founded year, HQ city, primary product URL, signup URL and access category are from general knowledge and were NOT individually verified in this pass; they are tagged "(unverified)". All access categories assume consumer apps with free browse/account (Category A) unless stated. Design-team and design-system evidence is "not checked" unless cited; "not checked" does not mean none exists. Today = 2026-11-17.
