### Inferences
- Large-scale lenders (Kissht, KreditBee, Fibe, Moneyview) have the multi-product + Android/iOS/web scale that fits DS consulting; Rs 1,000 Cr+ revenue and profitable, so budget exists.
- Loss-making/cash-constrained (Fi, Uni, Rupeek past) are lower-likelihood buyers.
- Brand/channel complexity (partners, co-lending, in-app credit lines + card) is the likely DS hook; this is inference, not confirmed.
