## PRIMARY candidates (product potentially accessible without sales)

### Takeaway
Best-evidenced candidates by scale and non-household status: Plum (B2B employee benefits, Series B 2026), Ditto (consumer advice+buy, profitable), Onsurity, Zopper, InsuranceDekho (larger, near-IPO), Loop Health, Pazcare, Riskcovry, Toffee. Access categories need hands-on verification because sites were unreachable.
