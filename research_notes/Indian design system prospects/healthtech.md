# Indian Healthtech Prospects for Layer One (design-systems consulting)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method: WebSearch only. WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (inc42.com, entrackr.com, eka.care, wysa.com: "ENOTFOUND", outside the session's allowed network). So NO signup/demo URL was opened or tested, and no careers page was read directly. Everything below is from search-result summaries. Marker legend: [S] = in a cited search result; [K] = from my prior knowledge, NOT verified in this session (treat as a lead); "ND" = not publicly disclosed / not found. Revenue = operating revenue unless stated. Aggregator figures (Inc42 profile pages, CB Insights, Latka, Bitscale, Clay, Zoominfo) conflict often; where I show a range, prefer RoC-filing-based Entrackr numbers.

## Takeaways for the report writer
- 18 PRIMARY candidates below (consumer-facing app or booking flow, no sales call needed by design) and 6 SECONDARY. Access category is inferred from product model and search snippets, not hands-on testing; the verification gap is the biggest caveat.
- Strongest design-system signals found: Eka Care (job posts say team maintains a design system used across EMR, PHR and AI flows) and Even Healthcare (hiring Product Designer UI).
- Clinic/hospital SaaS: Eka Care is the only one with a reported free tier for doctors (Dec 2025 blog "important update to its free tier"; current terms not read). HealthPlix: a directory says no free trial (demo/sales-led).
