questions:

Are the Lab tools free?

Yes. Every tool is free, needs no account and runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server.

Do the tools work together?

Yes. They share your work in this browser: a palette you make is available in the contrast checker and the token generator, and the token generator combines your palette, type, spacing and grid into one file.

Can I share what I've made?

Every tool has a “Copy link to this” button that encodes your settings in the link, so a colleague opens exactly what you see.

What formats can I export?

CSS custom properties, Tailwind config, SCSS and W3C DTCG JSON for tokens; Markdown, CSV and JSON for the planning tools.

Who made these tools?

Layer One, a design systems studio. They come from the method we use on client systems at companies like CARS24, IBM and JPMorgan Chase.
