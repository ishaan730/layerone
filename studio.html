A design systems studio in Bangalore, building the shared foundations product teams ship on.

Layer One was started by Aniket Pandey after six years inside design systems teams: first at IBM in Bangalore, then leading the CARS24 design system through a rebrand across three countries, and now on the design systems team at JPMorgan Chase.

The same problems came up everywhere. Teams didn't need another component kit. They needed decisions that hold across products, a way to run the system without becoming a bottleneck, and a reason for people to use it. That's the work Layer One exists to do, and it's the only work we do.
