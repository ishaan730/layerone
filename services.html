how it runs

    Audit

    Evidence first: files, code, accessibility and how work moves between teams.

  4. After

    Run

    Governance, docs and training, so the system keeps working without us.

questions

Why start with an audit?

Because the right answer depends on what you already have. Most teams have more working parts than they think, and a few problems causing most of the pain. The audit finds both, and its fee is credited if you go on to a Foundations Build within 60 days.

Do you only work on design systems?

Yes. No marketing sites, no app builds. Tokens, components, patterns, documentation and the governance around them.

Can you work with our existing system?

Yes. Most engagements improve or scale a system that exists. We keep what works.

Do you write code?

We pair with your engineers on token pipelines and component APIs, and can build components in React or as web components when you need extra hands.

Which tools?

Figma, Storybook, Tokens Studio, Style Dictionary and whatever your teams already use. The structure we design isn't tied to a tool.

Where are you based? Which time zones?

Bangalore, India (IST). We work with teams in India, the Middle East, Europe and the US, with at least two hours of overlap every day.

What does a founding partner commit to?

Being named as a client and working with us on a published case study once the work ships. In return you get 20% off and our full attention.
