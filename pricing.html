pricing questions:

Why fixed prices instead of hourly?

Because you should know the cost before you start. We agree scope, timeline and price up front. If something new comes up, we price it separately and you decide.

What does the founding-partner discount need from us?

Permission to name you as a client and to publish a case study together once the work ships. Three slots are open for engagements starting in 2027.

Is the Audit worth doing if we already know we need Foundations?

Foundations includes the audit in week one, so you don't need both. If you do the Audit first, its full fee is credited against Foundations within 60 days.

How do payments work?

Audit: in full at the start. Foundations: 50% to start, 50% on handover. Partner: monthly in advance, with no minimum beyond the first month. We invoice in US dollars.

Can we pause Partner?

Yes. Give us notice before the next billing date and we'll pause or stop. Everything we've made is already yours.

Do you build the code too?

We pair with your engineers on token pipelines and component APIs, and map every component to code. For larger builds in React or web components, we'll quote a component pack.

Our system is very large. Do these prices still apply?

These prices cover most product teams. For several systems, many brands or a large migration, we'll send a fixed quote after the call.

Not sure which one fits?

Tell us where your system is today. We'll recommend one, or tell you honestly if you don't need us yet.
